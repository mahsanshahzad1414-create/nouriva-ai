
🌿 Nouriva AI

AI-Assisted Nutrition Screening & Education Prototype

Nouriva AI is a student-built health technology project designed to support early nutrition awareness through artificial intelligence, simple screening tools, and nutrition education.

🎯 Problem

Millions of people worldwide experience nutrition challenges such as:

- Undernutrition
- Stunting
- Wasting
- Micronutrient deficiencies
- Food insecurity

Early awareness and education can help individuals understand potential nutrition risks.

💡 Solution

Nouriva AI provides a simple digital nutrition screening experience using:

- Age
- Height
- Weight
- BMI calculation
- Diet information
- Food access indicators
- Basic nutrition risk assessment

🚀 Current MVP Features

- ✅ BMI screening
- ✅ Nutrition risk indicators
- ✅ Diet and lifestyle assessment
- ✅ Nutrition education guidance
- ✅ User-friendly interface

🔬 Future Development

Future versions may include:

- AI nutrition assistant
- Food image analysis
- Body posture and growth assessment
- Malnutrition risk prediction
- Child growth monitoring
- Multilingual nutrition education
- Global malnutrition data visualization

🌍 Vision

To create an accessible AI-powered nutrition companion that helps improve nutrition awareness and supports global health goals.

⚠️ Disclaimer

Nouriva AI is a student prototype for nutrition education and preliminary screening. It does not diagnose medical conditions and does not replace professional healthcare advice.
